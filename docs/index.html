Glypican-3 across 33 cancers: a strong liver-cancer marker with a hidden link to survival

GPC3 is a cell-surface protein switched back on in liver cancer and a target for antibody and CAR-T therapies. In my diploma project (phase 1) I tested five cancers with web tools. Phase 2 rebuilds it as one reproducible pipeline on public data: every TCGA cancer against normal tissue, how well GPC3 alone spots a tumour, whether it predicts survival once stage and age are accounted for, what drives it, which genes move with it, and where cancer mutations sit on its structure.

Checks

    Methods

    • Expression: UCSC Xena Toil recompute of TCGA and GTEx (RSEM TPM, same pipeline for tumour and normal), converted to log2(TPM+1). Tumour = TCGA primary tumours; normal = TCGA adjacent normal plus GTEx samples of the same primary site. Mann–Whitney test, Benjamini–Hochberg FDR across cancers, AUC with 1,000 bootstrap resamples. Cancers with fewer than 10 normal samples are shown but not tested.
    • Survival: TCGA Clinical Data Resource endpoints (OS, DSS, PFI). Log-rank test on a median split; Cox models with GPC3 per standard deviation, alone and adjusted for age, sex and stage (I–II vs III–IV); Schoenfeld-residual test of proportional hazards. Cutpoints scanned from the 20th to 80th percentile; 1,000 permutations of GPC3 across patients give the chance distribution of the smallest p.
    • Drivers: GISTIC2 thresholded copy number and Illumina 450k methylation probes in GPC3 (TCGA-LIHC, UCSC Xena); Spearman correlations, Benjamini–Hochberg across probes.
    • Co-expression: Spearman correlation of GPC3 with every gene expressed in at least 20% of TCGA-LIHC tumours (RSEM, log2(x+1)); pre-ranked GSEA (gseapy, 1,000 permutations) on the 50 MSigDB Hallmark gene sets.
    • Structure: UniProt P51654 features; AlphaFold DB model and per-residue pLDDT; somatic mutations in the TCGA PanCancer Atlas from cBioPortal; binomial test of missense mutations in confidently folded residues (pLDDT ≥ 70) against their share of the protein.
    • Reproducible: python run_all.py runs every step; a GitHub workflow runs it end to end with unit tests (AUC, Benjamini–Hochberg, log-rank against lifelines, cutpoint permutation on simulated null data) and checks on the output. Seeds are fixed.

    What this cannot tell you

    • TCGA adjacent normal tissue is not healthy tissue, and GTEx donors differ from patients; the TCGA-only comparison is a check on that.
    • RNA level is not protein on the cell surface, which is what antibodies and CAR-T cells see.
    • Survival models use one cohort with treatment and follow-up that vary between centres; no external validation cohort is included.
    • Correlation with copy number, methylation or pathways is not causation.

    Data: UCSC Xena (TCGA, GTEx, TCGA Clinical Data Resource), cBioPortal (TCGA PanCancer Atlas), UniProt, AlphaFold DB, MSigDB via Enrichr. Code: github.com/bymankirat/GPC3-Pan-Cancer-Biomarker. Built by Mankirat Singh.
